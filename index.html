5 · Strategic Blind Spots

⚠️ Single Point of Failure

Mac = media studio. VPS = hub. Either goes down, revenue stops. Need backup Mac, secondary VPS, or failover plan before accepting paying customers.

⚠️ Unfinished Backups

Asset-Manager repo (3 years of work) still not git-backed. Flagged in Claude Notes Six AND Seven. Before monetizing: secure your IP. A paying customer discovering unprotected source destroys the "verifiable" brand.

⚠️ Solo Developer Bandwidth

Five revenue paths, one developer. Arena and Studio can run semi-automated, but API and Certification require developer relations and enterprise sales. Consider which paths can wait.

⚠️ Market Timing

AI agent platforms exploding. Window to establish FRAYMUS as the "verifiable agent registry" is now. But market may not yet know it needs agent verification. Demand validation needed.
